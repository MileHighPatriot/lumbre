Order tacos

Cooked for the room, not for a template.

The menu is the one a Denver dining room actually orders from: pastor and birria, enchiladas in four sauces, a mole that is a sauce, fajitas, aguas, and a short bar. The look is newer. The food is not a costume.

  1. 1. Build the ticket. Heat, fillings, and add-ons change the price before it hits the order.
  2. 2. Pick a window. Tuesday and Thursday prices follow the time you choose, up to a week out.
  3. 3. Pay at the window. Or pay the driver. No card is charged here.
