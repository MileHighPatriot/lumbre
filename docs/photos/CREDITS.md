# Menu photo credits

Every menu item that did not already have a picture now has one. These 68 photos were generated for this concept site so each picture could match that dish.

They are original images, not stock photos. There is no Unsplash or Pexels source URL for them. The license is the same idea as those licenses: commercial use is allowed, and no attribution is required.

No photo shows an identifiable face, a hand, or a real restaurant brand. The cola and the four lager bottles are unbranded stand-ins. They are not photographs of Coca-Cola, Modelo Especial, Negra Modelo, Pacífico, or Victoria.

Each full frame is WebP, cropped 3:2, and under 80 KB. The color is lightened and warmed toward the older photos: brighter, with a peach cast. The menu list does not load those full frames. It loads a 160 pixel square and a 240 pixel square from `public/photos/thumbs/`, and the browser picks one with srcset. The sizes are 76px on a phone and 120px from 640px up.
